ALARM SOUND FILE NEEDED
========================

Please add an MP3 file named "alarm_sound.mp3" to this directory.

For the loudest and most effective alarm, use an audio file with:
- High volume normalization
- Sharp, attention-grabbing tones (sirens, beeps, or loud music)
- Duration of at least 30 seconds (it will loop)
- MP3 format for iOS compatibility

You can use:
- A siren sound effect
- A loud beeping tone
- Upbeat, energetic music
- Any sound that will effectively wake you up

The app is configured to play this sound at maximum volume with continuous looping
and vibration until dismissed or snoozed.
