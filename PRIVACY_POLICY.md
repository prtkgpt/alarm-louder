# Privacy Policy for Alarm Louder

**Last Updated: December 25, 2024**

## Introduction

Alarm Louder ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device
- **Alarm Settings**: Times, labels, repeat schedules, and snooze configurations
- **Coin Balance**: Number of coins available for snoozing
- **App Preferences**: User settings and configurations

**Important**: All this data is stored ONLY on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this information on external servers.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Device information
- Usage analytics
- Contacts or photos
- Any data that leaves your device

## In-App Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packages) are processed entirely by Apple Inc. through the App Store:
- We do not receive or store any payment information
- We do not access credit card or payment details
- All transactions are subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Purchase receipts are verified through Apple's servers

## Data Storage and Security

- All app data is stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage (UserDefaults)
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- No user accounts or cloud sync
- Data is protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, etc.)

## Notifications

The app requires notification permissions to trigger alarms when the app is not active:
- Notifications are generated locally on your device
- No notification data is collected or transmitted
- You can revoke notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time

## Audio Playback

The app requires audio playback permissions to play alarm sounds:
- Audio is played locally on your device
- No audio is recorded or transmitted
- Background audio mode ensures alarms work when app is not in foreground

## Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on our website with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Data Deletion

To delete all app data:
1. Delete the Alarm Louder app from your device
2. All locally stored data (alarms, coins, preferences) will be permanently removed

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data:
- There is no data to access, modify, or delete on our servers
- All your data remains under your control on your device
- You can clear app data by deleting and reinstalling the app
